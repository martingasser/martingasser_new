Co-Corporeality@Angewandte Festival 2021

For the Co-Corporeality artistic research project, we have developed a mobile eye tracking solution. It consists of a Pupil Labs eye camera, an Intel Realsense T265 tracking camera, and an Intel Realsense D435 world camera, mounted on a bike helmet. A Raspberry Pi 4 is used to compress and transmit the image data over Ethernet to another computer where a combination of the Pupil Labs software and custom software components is used to determine the exact position and gaze direction of a viewer in space. This information can be subsequently used to detect which objects a person currently looks at.
